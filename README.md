# Indian Smartphone Market Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing smartphone sales, product performance, and customer behavior across major Indian cities.

## Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of smartphone sales performance and customer preferences in the Indian market.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall sales performance and trends
- Brand-wise and model-wise product analysis
- Customer ratings and satisfaction
- Payment method preferences
- City-wise transaction and revenue distribution

## Key Business Insights

- **Top-Selling Model:** iPhone SE emerged as the leading smartphone model, with 60M units sold.
- **Leading Cities:** Delhi and Mumbai recorded the highest transaction volumes.
- **Monthly Sales Performance:** July was the best-performing month, generating 69.6M in sales, while September recorded the lowest sales at 56.9M.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** The average customer rating stands at 3.69 out of 5, indicating opportunities to improve customer experience.
- **Customer Preferences:** The dashboard provides insights into payment method preferences and smartphone purchasing behavior.
